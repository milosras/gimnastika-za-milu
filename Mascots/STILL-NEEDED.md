## If a result comes back wrong

- **On a background instead of transparent, or with a patch of it still stuck
  to her:** *"Same images but with a fully transparent background — PNG with
  alpha, nothing behind the character at all, no leftover patches, clean edge
  around the fur."*
- **Cut off at the edge:** *"Zoom out — the whole body from ears to toes must be
  inside the frame with a margin."*
- **Drifting off-model:** attach one more reference from the same folder and say
  *"keep the face, fur colour and leotard exactly as in the references."*
- **Cobra keeps coming back sitting up:** *"She is lying face down on the floor.
  Her hips and back legs stay flat on the ground; only her chest and head lift."*
